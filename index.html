How the trust score works

The score runs from 0 to 100. It starts at 50 when TrustCheck knows nothing, and moves with each signal it can confirm or contradict.

  1. Only businesses whose trade licence a reviewer has checked count as registered. An exact match with their handle, phone, website or name raises the score to about 90. If the licence has expired, the score stays at 55.
  2. A handle or site that imitates a registered business, such as an extra "_official" or "offers", drops it to about 25.
  3. A phone number or payment link that differs from the one the business registered takes off 30 to 35 points.
  4. A photo, or any frame of a video, that is at least 85% similar to another business's registered original marks the account as using stolen content.
  5. Accounts and numbers that reviewers confirmed in user reports are capped at 12.
70 – 100Strong Match. The details match a registered business.
36 – 69Unverified. Ask for proof before paying.
0 – 35High Risk. Don't send money.

A strong match means that the details match a registered business. It does not guarantee that the business is completely safe. Every result is an initial risk assessment, not a guarantee of safety. Always verify payment details through the official business account before paying.

Privacy

Photos and videos you check never leave this browser. TrustCheck shrinks each image to a 768-bit fingerprint (horizontal and vertical difference hashes plus an average hash) and compares fingerprints, so a cropped, brightened or re-saved copy still matches. For a video, it takes 12 still frames spread across the clip (up to the first 3 minutes) and fingerprints each frame the same way.

Shared data: verified businesses (their public details, photo fingerprints and small previews) and accounts confirmed in reports are shared with everyone, live. A business's licence photo is visible only to the applicant and the reviewers, and is deleted once the review is done. Your saved reports are visible only to you and the reviewers, and reviewers do not see who sent them. Customers can check without an account and without sharing anything.
